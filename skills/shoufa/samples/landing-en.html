It shows you the plan first

Before each task, Relay lists the steps it will take. It starts once you have looked. When something is unclear, it stops and asks.

  • Every step is logged, so you can look back
  • Paying, sending or deleting always needs your OK
  • Run a task once, repeat it with one sentence
  1. Open your inbox and find October's receipts
  2. Read the amount, date and vendor on each
  3. Match them line by line to the bank statement
  4. Stop on anything that doesn't match, and show you
  5. Fill in your company's template, then wait for your OK
